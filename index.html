Our Namesake

In Honor of Peter Callais

A native of Golden Meadow and a resident of Cut Off, La., Callais was a respected member of the local business community. He served on the board of directors and as secretary of Community Bank in Raceland and on the board of directors of Solid Waste Disposal Inc. in Houma. He was the 2000 chairman of the Chamber of Lafourche and Bayou Region, the Louisiana Department of Economic Development's 1999 Small Business Person of the Year for District Three, and a 2001 Louisiana and Mississippi Ernst and Young Entrepreneur of the Year. His business had also been listed among the 500 Fastest Growing Companies in America in 1998, 1999, 2000, 2002 and 2007.

In 2025 the Callais Family endowed this institute in his memory — leadership, innovation, and service, carried forward through data.
