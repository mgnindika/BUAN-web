About

Analytics Entrepreneurship at LOYNO

The Peter Callais Institute Tech Startup Program supports students and recent graduates who want to build analytics-driven companies. Whether you're building a SaaS tool, a data marketplace, an AI application, or an analytics consulting practice, this program gives you the structure, mentorship, and resources to move from idea to launch.

The program runs in two tracks: a semester-long Incubator Track for early-stage ideas, and a year-long Accelerator Track for ventures with initial traction seeking their first customers or investment.

All participants receive access to cloud computing credits (AWS, GCP, Azure), software licenses, co-working space at the Institute, and a dedicated mentor from the Institute's industry advisory network.

INCUBATOR TRACK

1 semester · Idea to MVP · Open to all students

ACCELERATOR TRACK

1 year · MVP to market · By application only

What Participants Receive

Everything you need to test, build, and launch — without the startup overhead.

🧑‍🏫

1-on-1 Mentorship

Each team is matched with an industry mentor from our advisory network — entrepreneurs, VCs, and analytics executives who meet bi-weekly.

☁️

Cloud Credits

$5,000–$25,000 in AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure credits through the Institute's university partnerships. Build at scale from day one.

💰

Seed Grants

Accelerator Track teams are eligible for a $5,000 no-equity seed grant funded by the Institute's Entrepreneurship Fund.

🏢

Co-Working Space

Dedicated desk space and private meeting rooms in the Callais Institute for the duration of your program participation.

🔗

Investor Access

Demo Day each spring connects Accelerator Track teams with Gulf South angel investors and regional venture capital firms.

📚

Curriculum & Workshops

Monthly workshops on legal, finance, product, and marketing — tailored for analytics and data companies. No generic startup advice.
