Our Mission

Democratizing Data Literacy

The Callais Institute believes that data literacy is a civic skill — as fundamental as reading and writing for participation in 21st-century society. That's why we invest in open educational resources that any instructor, at any level, can use without cost.

Our Data Science Education initiative develops and distributes curricula, datasets, project templates, and pedagogical frameworks for teaching analytics. Resources are designed for high school teachers, community college instructors, and university faculty.

We curate the best free, open educational resources available — from university labs, nonprofits, and open-source projects — so any instructor can teach data science without cost.

K–12 Outreach

Bringing Analytics into Schools

The Institute partners directly with New Orleans and Louisiana K–12 schools to bring data literacy programming into classrooms. Our K–12 education team provides professional development workshops for teachers, guest classroom visits, and structured curriculum support.

We prioritize schools in historically underserved communities, with particular focus on Title I schools in Orleans and Jefferson parishes.

  • ✓ Teacher PD workshops (full-day and half-day formats)
  • ✓ LOYNO student guest instructors for classroom visits
  • ✓ Curriculum co-design with your school's CS or math department
  • ✓ Free access to all Callais Institute educational materials
  • ✓ Pipeline to the AAI4HC summer program for top students

Pedagogical Research

Journal Article

Advising Student-Driven Analytics Projects: A Summary of Experiences and Lessons Learned

Babier, A., Fernandes, C., Zhu, I.Y. · INFORMS Transactions on Education · 2023

Analyzes 58 undergraduate analytics capstone projects and 106 student surveys to identify common obstacles in student-driven machine learning, optimization, and simulation projects, and how faculty advising resolved them.

Journal Article

Reframing Data Ethics in Research Methods Education: A Pathway to Critical Data Literacy

Atenas, J., Havemann, L., Timmermann, C. · Int'l Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education · 2023

Synthesizes a review of 250 research methods syllabi, 80 data science programs, and 12 data ethics frameworks to propose an ethics framework that goes beyond informed consent to address power structures and protection of vulnerable populations.

Beyond the Numbers: K–12 Teachers' Experiences, Beliefs, and Challenges in Developing Data Literacy

Khor, E.T., Chee, A., Lee, S.S. · Education Sciences · 2025

A qualitative study of how K–12 teachers perceive and use data literacy in daily practice, identifying barriers including time constraints, limited confidence, and insufficient professional support.
