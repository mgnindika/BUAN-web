Our Mission

What We Stand For

The Peter Callais Institute for Business Analytics at Loyola University New Orleans is committed to honoring Peter Callais' legacy of leadership, innovation, and service. We educate and empower students, professionals, and organizations through rigorous analytics training, interdisciplinary collaboration, and industry partnerships. Guided by Jesuit values, we cultivate ethical, innovative, and socially responsible leaders who use the power of data to advance business, strengthen communities, and inspire positive change.
Our Vision

Where We Are Going

To be a nationally recognized hub for ethical and impactful business analytics education and research — shaping future leaders who harness data to drive innovation, foster inclusion, and create transformative solutions for business and society.
What We Believe

Our Core Values

Six principles that guide everything we do — from curriculum design to community engagement.

Innovation

Embracing new ideas and emerging technologies to solve problems that matter to Louisiana and the world.

Ethics & Integrity

Responsible, transparent, and principled data decision-making at every level of our work.

Collaboration

Partnerships across disciplines, industries, and communities — because great analytics rarely happens alone.

Community Impact

Analytics as a force for social good — applying our skills to real challenges in the Gulf South.

Data-Driven Excellence

Rigorous methods, reproducible research, and actionable insights grounded in statistical best practices.

Jesuit Values

Education of the whole person — cura personalis, intellectual excellence, and service to the common good.
