Research at the Institute

Research Spotlights

A look at the research questions our faculty and students are actively working on.

Research Focus

Biomedical & Veterinary Text Processing

Dr. Nuwan Indika's work applies natural language processing and text analytics to complex human and veterinary medicine data, developing new methods to extract structured insight from unstructured clinical text.

Research Focus

Market Structure & Vertical Mergers

Applied econometric analysis of vertical mergers in the soft drink industry, examining how consolidation up and down the supply chain affects pricing, competition, and market outcomes.

Research Focus

Sports Analytics

A cross-institutional research collaboration applying statistical and machine learning methods to sports performance and strategy questions, developed with a faculty partner at Syracuse University.

Research Focus

Data Consortium Collaboration

Institute faculty participate in the 1DATA Consortium, a multi-institution research initiative anchored at Kansas State University, building shared data infrastructure and methods across health-related research domains.
