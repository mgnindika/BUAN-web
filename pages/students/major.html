Program Overview

Why Business Analytics?

Business Analytics is one of the fastest-growing fields in the global economy. Organizations of every size and sector need professionals who can collect, analyze, and communicate data-driven insights — and act on them ethically and effectively.

Loyola University New Orleans offers a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) with a major in Business Analytics through the AACSB-accredited College of Business. The program uniquely combines quantitative rigor with Jesuit ethical reasoning — producing analysts who are both technically excellent and morally grounded.

The major is available as a primary degree or as a double major, making it an excellent complement to Finance, Marketing, Computer Science, Economics, Management, and more.

Core Curriculum

A rigorous blend of business, statistics, programming, and ethics — designed to produce versatile analytics professionals.

BUAN B205

Business Analytics

Foundational business analytics course; requires Sophomore standing

COSC A211

Intro to Programming I

Introductory programming, part of the major's mathematics/CS adjunct requirements

MATH A200

Intro to Linear Algebra

Linear algebra foundations required for the major

BUAN B305

Business Decision Modeling

Requires BUAN B205 and MGT B250; Junior standing

BUAN B310

Decision Support Systems

Requires COSC A211, COSC A208 or MGT B250 or ACCT B340; Junior standing

BUAN B360

Econometrics I — Linear

Requires BUAN B205, ECON B201; Junior standing

LGST B105

Business Ethics

Requires BA B100, LGST B105; Senior standing
